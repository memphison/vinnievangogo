The Legend

The pizzeria with the guy on the sign.

You've walked past him a hundred times. Wild eyes, a chef's hat, and a slice hanging out of his mouth. That's Vinnie. The name's a terrible pun, but the pizza's good enough that nobody minds.

New York-style pizza starts with dough mixed fresh every night. Every pie is topped with mozzarella, fresh-grated parmesan, and the same familiar sauce that's kept people coming back for decades. Grab a whole pie or stop at the walk-up window for a slice the size of your face.

🚲

Fun fact: Vinnie's has delivered pizza around downtown Savannah by bicycle. Somewhere out there, a hot pie is currently being pedaled past a horse carriage.
